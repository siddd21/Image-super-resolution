# Low-Light Image Denoising & Super-Resolution - Kaggle Competition

This repository contains my solution for the [Low-Light Image Denoising & Super-Resolution](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/dlp-may-2025-nppe-3/overview) competition on Kaggle.

---

## Competition Overview
The goal of this competition is to **develop robust deep learning models capable of denoising and enhancing the resolution of low-light images**.  
Participants are provided with a dataset of noisy, low-resolution images and are tasked with producing visually clear, high-quality outputs with improved detail and resolution.

Specifically, the challenge involves:
- **Denoising**: Removing noise and artifacts from low-light images  
- **4× Super-Resolution**: Increasing the resolution by a factor of 4 while preserving textures and edges
